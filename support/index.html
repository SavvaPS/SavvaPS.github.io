Support

Need help or found a bug? Email gymsmith.app@gmail.com. It helps to include your phone model, your iOS or Android version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. GymSmith has no accounts and no servers, so we never see your workouts. See the privacy policy for the details.

How do I back up my workouts?

On iPhone: open Settings (the gear at the top of the app) and leave iCloud backup switched on. GymSmith then saves a copy to your own iCloud Drive once a day and after each workout, keeping the last 30 days.

On any phone: in Settings, under Backups, tap Back up now to save a backup file. Keep it somewhere safe, such as Google Drive, the Files app or an email to yourself.

How do I move my workouts to a new phone?

iPhone to iPhone: make sure a recent iCloud backup exists on your old phone. On the new phone, sign in to the same Apple account, install GymSmith, then open Settings and choose Restore from iCloud.

Android, or switching between iPhone and Android: on the old phone, go to Settings → Backups → Back up now and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. Install GymSmith on the new phone, then go to Settings → Backups → Import backup and choose that file.

I bought the unlock but the app doesn't show it

Use Restore purchase in the app while signed in to the Apple account or Google account you bought it with. Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so we can't see or refund them. For refunds on iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund help.

Can I export my history?

Yes. You can export your history as a spreadsheet (CSV) or save a backup file, and keep it wherever you like.

The rest timer alert doesn't appear when my phone is locked

On iPhone: check that notifications are allowed for GymSmith in Settings → Notifications. Focus modes can also hide them.

On Android: allow notifications for GymSmith, and allow Alarms & reminders so the alert arrives on time. If it's missing, GymSmith shows an Allow button in its Settings. Battery saver and Do Not Disturb can also delay or hide alerts.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Clear data, or delete the app. We hold no copy, so deleted data can't be recovered unless you kept a backup.
