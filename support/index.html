Support

Need help or found a bug? Email gymsmith.app@gmail.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. GymSmith has no accounts and no servers, so we never see your workouts. See the privacy policy for the details.

How do I back up my workouts?

Open Settings (the gear at the top of the app) and leave iCloud backup switched on. GymSmith then saves a copy to your own iCloud Drive once a day and after each workout, keeping the last 30 days. Tap Back up now to make one straight away.

How do I move my workouts to a new phone?

Make sure a recent iCloud backup exists on your old phone. On the new phone, sign in to the same Apple account, install GymSmith, then open Settings and choose Restore from iCloud.

I bought the unlock but the app doesn't show it

Use Restore purchase in the app while signed in to the Apple account you bought it with. Purchases are handled by Apple, so we can't see or refund them. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I export my history?

Yes. You can export your history as a spreadsheet (CSV) or save a backup file, and keep it wherever you like.

The rest timer alert doesn't appear when my phone is locked

Check that notifications are allowed for GymSmith in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications. Focus modes can also hide them.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Clear data, or delete the app. We hold no copy, so deleted data can't be recovered unless you kept a backup.
